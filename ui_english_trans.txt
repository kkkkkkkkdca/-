Bonus Turn!
<u>Battles:</u> Hit an enemy's weak point for more damage and to fill up the star-shaped meter on the upper left to gain an additional bonus turn. Hitting weaknesses also pushes back an enemy's next turn. Attacking foes from behind or from above also does more damage, as well as utilizing a character's attack type well (slice, blunt and projectiles). Target an enemy to see its weaknesses.
<u>Using skills on surroundings:</u> To progress, you may sometimes need to use a skill on hotspots marked with ''!''. Select ''Use Here'' from a character's skill menu to do so.
<u>Skills:</u> You'll find skills and spells during your adventure. Open a character's skill menu and equip skills in the ''learn'' slot to be able to use them. After gaining enough learning points (LP) in a battle, the character will learn the skill permanently.
<u>Exploration:</u> Your surroundings will hide useful items and treasures. Keep an eye out for them. Follow the ? icon in the mini radar to progress the story.
<u>Saving:</u> The game doesn't save automatically. Please save manually from the menu.
